Healthy Recipes

Vegetable Salad

Ingredients: Cucumber, tomato, carrot, lemon.

Preparation: Chop vegetables and mix with lemon juice.

Calories: About 100 kcal per serving.

Oats Breakfast

Ingredients: Oats, milk, banana and nuts.

Preparation: Cook oats in milk and add banana.

Calories: About 300 kcal per serving.

Dal and Rice

Ingredients: Rice, dal, vegetables and spices.

Preparation: Cook rice and dal and serve together.

Calories: About 350 kcal per serving.

Healthy Eating Tips

  • Drink enough water throughout the day.
  • Eat fresh fruits and vegetables.
  • Include protein in your meals.
  • Reduce excess sugar and salt.
  • Eat meals at regular times.
  • Get enough sleep and exercise regularly.
